# OMAZ MUSIC — Repeatable Video Production Workflow

This is the working production standard for OMAZ MUSIC. It is designed to reduce repeated planning and make every upload follow the same reliable process. It does not claim that third-party sites have been automated unless a confirmed integration performs the action.

## Brand rules (apply every time)

- Channel promise: calm music for studying, working, reading, relaxing and unwinding.
- Visual direction: bright, inviting, cinematic Quiet Luxury. Use natural light, golden hour, cream/ivory, sage green, sky blue, soft teal and warm beige.
- Preferred settings: elegant cafés, sunlit minimalist apartments, coastal views, gardens, scenic trains and peaceful modern interiors.
- Avoid dark, gloomy, cluttered or generic bedroom imagery. Keep the composition clean and legible at mobile thumbnail size.
- Sound signature: ultra-soft cinematic ambient lo-fi, warm Rhodes and felt piano, minimal percussion, usually 58–70 BPM where appropriate; no hi-hats or snare for the core signature.
- Do not copy competitors’ artwork, titles or branding. Learn from their audience promise and presentation, then make an original version.
- Target album length: 1h50m–1h59m; usually 20–25 tracks when making a multi-track album, subject to the actual music available.

## One-video intake

Create one folder per release with:
- `01-research.md` — audience intent, search phrases, competing videos and observations.
- `02-music-prompts.md` — master sound prompt and track-by-track variations.
- `03-artwork.md` — thumbnail prompt, visual direction and any asset credits.
- `04-metadata.md` — final title, description, chapters/timestamps if useful, tags if useful, playlist and pinned comment.
- `05-QA.md` — export and upload checklist.
- Final audio, thumbnail, video export and project file.

## Step 1 — Demand and competitor research

Before selecting a title:
1. Search YouTube for the exact listening use case and genre (examples: `lofi music for studying`, `smooth jazz and R&B for relaxation`, `chill beats for working`).
2. Inspect relevant results, prioritizing videos with meaningful view counts relative to channel size and recent uploads. Record title wording, duration, thumbnail subject, audience promise and visible engagement.
3. Separate evidence from guesses. Do not label a phrase “high volume” unless a keyword tool or first-party analytics supports that claim.
4. Select one primary search intent and one clear differentiator for this upload. Do not mix unrelated genres in one title.

## Step 2 — Title and thumbnail

Prepare three genuinely different title candidates, each with:
- Primary search phrase near the beginning where natural.
- Clear listening purpose or genre.
- A specific reason to click that accurately reflects the music.
- A matching thumbnail concept.

Prefer clear examples such as:
- `Smooth Jazz & R&B for Relaxation | Warm Saxophone, Rhodes & Chill Grooves`
- `Lo-Fi Music for Studying and Deep Focus | Soft Piano & Warm Rhodes`
These are examples only, not validated keyword rankings. Choose the final title only after checking actual competing results.

Thumbnail rules:
- 16:9, 1280 × 720.
- One strong focal subject or destination; bright, attractive lighting and clean negative space.
- At most a few large words if text improves recognition. Avoid small text, fake badges and crowded collages.
- Ensure title and thumbnail complement one another rather than repeating the same words.
- Use the OMAZ MUSIC identity consistently without overpowering the scene.

## Step 3 — Music generation

Use a stable master prompt and vary only the track's mood/instrument emphasis. Core prompt:

“Create original ultra-soft cinematic ambient lo-fi for OMAZ MUSIC. Warm Rhodes chords and felt piano, gentle melodic movement, restrained bass, minimal soft percussion, calm spacious production, natural dynamics, seamless listening, no vocals unless explicitly requested, no hi-hats, no snare. Avoid abrupt transitions, harsh transients, excessive brightness and dramatic drops. Aim for a peaceful, polished sound suitable for studying, reading, working or unwinding. Tempo: [choose 58–70 BPM when appropriate]. Mood and variation: [describe one distinct idea].”

For jazz/R&B releases, make a separate prompt that explicitly specifies the desired smooth-jazz/R&B arrangement and instruments; do not force the ambient lo-fi prompt onto a different genre. Confirm licensing and commercial-use rights for the chosen generator and any samples.

## Step 4 — Artwork and motion

Artwork prompt template:

“Create an original bright cinematic Quiet Luxury scene for OMAZ MUSIC, 16:9 composition. [Specific destination or setting] in beautiful natural light, refined cream and warm beige with touches of sage green/soft teal/sky blue, elegant composition with breathing space, premium editorial illustration or cinematic realism, strong focal point readable at thumbnail size, subtle atmospheric depth. Calm, inviting and optimistic. No dark gloomy mood, no clutter, no logos, no copied characters or recognizable copyrighted artwork. Leave clear negative space for optional short title text.”

For a long music video, use a clean visual loop or restrained slow movement; avoid distracting rapid cuts. Verify loop seams, visual quality, and that no generated text artifacts appear. Keep the music central.

## Step 5 — Assemble and export

1. Import only the approved final music files and selected visual.
2. Build a single continuous timeline; place tracks in the intended order and check transitions/crossfades.
3. Check loudness consistency and listen to the start, several middle points, every transition and the end.
4. For a looping visual, inspect the loop seam and avoid sudden jumps.
5. Export at 1920 × 1080 or higher, 16:9, using a YouTube-compatible MP4/H.264 setting and high-quality audio.
6. Watch the exported file locally from beginning to end or sample it systematically before upload. Never assume an export succeeded just because a render was started.

## Step 6 — Upload package

Prepare metadata before opening YouTube Studio:
- Title: chosen after real search/competitor review.
- Description: first two lines clearly state the genre and use case; then provide tracklist/timestamps if available, listening suggestions and accurate credits/licensing details.
- Playlist: choose the closest existing functional playlist (LoFi Music, Study Music, Sleep Music, Relaxing Music, Ambient Music) and, when appropriate, one story/destination playlist.
- Tags: add only relevant supporting terms; do not spend disproportionate effort on tags.
- Thumbnail: upload the final custom 1280 × 720 image.
- Audience setting and visibility: choose correctly; start as private/unlisted for review if uncertain.
- Do not publish or schedule publicly without the user's explicit approval.

## Step 7 — Learn from results

Review YouTube Studio after enough data accumulates:
- Impressions and click-through rate, with caution when sample sizes are small.
- Average view duration and retention curve, especially the first 30–60 seconds and track transitions.
- Traffic sources and actual search terms.
- Subscribers gained and returning viewers where available.

Change one major variable at a time when possible. Do not draw conclusions from a handful of impressions or views. Keep a short note of what was tested and what happened.

## Automation boundaries

- Reusable prompts, metadata drafts, QA lists and release folders can be prepared in advance.
- Canva can be used for reusable artwork layouts; autofill requires named fields configured in the design or a compatible brand template.
- VideoExpress, music-generation tools and YouTube publishing must be driven through a confirmed supported integration or their UI. Do not claim unattended execution, completed rendering, upload, scheduling or publication without a tool confirmation.
- Preserve original projects. Duplicate a project before destructive edits. Always request approval before public release.

## Definition of done

A release is ready for approval only when:
- Search intent and competitor notes are recorded.
- Music rights are checked and the full audio has been reviewed.
- Thumbnail is original, bright, legible and consistent with OMAZ MUSIC.
- Export is verified and metadata is complete.
- The project and final files are saved.
- Publication status is explicitly reported; nothing is public without approval.
