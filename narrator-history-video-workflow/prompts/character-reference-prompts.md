# Character reference prompts
Attach the approved image wherever supported. Reuse the identity block unchanged. Prompts cannot guarantee identity consistency; inspect every output.

## Fixed identity block
Use the attached concept image as the sole design reference for the same 3D character. Preserve its oversized red/pink mouth, white teeth and pink tongue; mismatched blue and green eyes in the same left/right positions; tall black top hat silhouette; head/body proportions, face layout, materials, key colors and whimsical theatrical 3D finish. Change only pose, expression, role wardrobe or scene. Keep eyes and mouth visible. No extra eyes/mouths, replacement hat, photorealism or flat 2D.

## Model sheet
[IDENTITY BLOCK] Show the same character at matching scale in front, three-quarter, side and back views on a plain background. Neutral standing pose, arms slightly away from body, resting expression. Consistent camera, scale, light and materials. No captions, props or other characters. Compare to source before accepting.

## Expression/viseme sheet
[IDENTITY BLOCK] Equally sized panels for neutral, explaining, curious, surprised, skeptical, concerned, amused and silent rest. Add mouth row: rest, M-B-P, F-V, wide vowel, rounded vowel, open vowel and tongue-forward TH/L. Keep teeth, tongue, eye colors and face geometry consistent.

## Wardrobe
Use [IDENTITY BLOCK]. Same model in [SCHOLARLY / EXPLORER / THEATRICAL] wardrobe for [VARIANT]. Keep hat and face visible. Accessories cannot change silhouette. Full-body front and three-quarter views on plain background. Costume changes only.

## Scene still
[IDENTITY BLOCK] Role: [VARIANT]. Verified period and place: [DATES, PLACE]. Visible action: [ONE ACTION]. Supporting objects/people: [RESEARCHED DETAILS]. Shot: [FRAMING]. Mood: [SUBJECT-SUITED TONE]. Keep eyes, mouth and hat readable; leave space for editor-added titles. No pseudo-text, logo, duplicate narrator or unsupported detail. For dialogue face camera; cutaways may omit narrator.

## Image-to-video
Animate accepted still without redesign. Preserve face, eye colors/positions, mouth, teeth, tongue, hat, body, costume, light and composition. Action: [ONE CONTROLLED MOTION]. Camera: [LOCKED OR SIMPLE MOVE]. For dialogue, use final narration as timing source and animate natural mouth shapes with consonant closures and pauses. Add no text, props, characters, cuts, costume changes or unsourced details. If the tool cannot sync audio, do not call it lip-synced; use it as silent coverage.

Negative constraints: no changed eyes, small/missing mouth, missing/multiplied teeth, disappearing tongue, extra eyes/mouths, replaced hat, covered face, body drift, costume flicker, unrelated style, malformed hands, duplicate character, generated text or unsupported historical props.
